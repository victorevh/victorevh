<h2>About Me</h2>
<p>
Full-stack engineer with 5 years of continuous growth at <strong>Hand Talk</strong>, building accessibility technology used across 1,000+ client websites and 15M+ users per year.
</p>
<p>
I currently split my time across three fronts: Senior engineering at Hand Talk, Co-Founder &amp; CTO at <strong>Gestur.io</strong> (a GovTech platform for municipal tourism I built end-to-end, live in 3 Brazilian municipalities), and Backend Tech Lead at <strong>NEES/UFAL</strong>, leading the backend for CultBR, a national platform tied to Brazil's Ministry of Culture.
</p>
<p>
My engineering approach is grounded in <strong>Clean Architecture</strong>, SOLID, and Feature-Sliced Design, and increasingly shaped by agentic AI workflows (Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot) and Spec-Driven Development.
</p>
